# Future Features

The following capabilities are **not part of the current foundation phase**.
They are documented here as aspirational goals for later iterations.

## Planned (no order implied)

- **Food-image recognition** — identify meals from photos using computer vision
- **Barcode scanning** — look up packaged foods via their barcode
- **Grocery lists** — auto-generated shopping lists based on meal plans
- **Pantry tracking** — manage on-hand ingredients and expiration dates
- **Wearable integration** — sync activity, sleep, and biometric data from wearables
- **Mobile application** — native or cross-platform mobile clients
- **Voice assistant** — hands-free interaction for logging and queries
- **Multilingual support** — localised UI and food databases
- **Dietitian portal** — professional dashboard for client management
- **Community recipes** — user-submitted and rated recipe sharing
- **Advanced ML personalisation** — adaptive recommendations based on user behaviour

> Anything listed here is a **stretch goal** and should not influence the
> current architectural decisions beyond keeping them extensible.
